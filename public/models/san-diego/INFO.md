# San Diego runtime model

This folder contains the static generated Torrey Highlands neighborhood model. Its mapped coverage is approximately 3.06 km wide and 3.06 km tall, expanded by 31% in width and 37.5% in height around the existing camera to conceal the previously visible geographic edges. Actual OSM buildings, streets, and landcover populate the perimeter. There are no direct subfolders.

- `torrey-highlands.glb` contains sage-green terrain, richer park greens and dry coastal scrub, cream-stucco buildings with terracotta roofs, real OSM streets and paths, deterministic low-poly trees, and four single-mesh selectable landmarks: Westview High School, Torrey Highlands Plaza, Mesa Verde Middle School, and Deer Canyon Elementary School. Mesa Verde’s western sports field adds a north-south cream oval track with a green infield and surrounding lawn based on the supplied visual reference, merged with Mesa Verde’s buildings, roofs, and windows so it moves with the school. Roads and parking keep distinct paved colors. All landmark facade details and roofs are baked into their corresponding base mesh. Mesa Verde and Deer Canyon additionally contain subdued differentiated roofs, roof-edge trim, framed windows, contained roof vents, entrances, supported shade canopies and seats, and modest campus paths/court surfaces (plus Deer Canyon play space). Their details and ground additions stay within their individual school meshes, preserving eight meshes and four selectable roots. These are artistic approximations on mapped buildings; existing athletics-track dimensions and colors remain unchanged. The GLB uses Meshopt compression with lossless Float32 positions to preserve centimeter gaps between road, shoulder, landcover, and roof layers. It has no textures or runtime map-service dependency.
- `INFO.md` is this folder guide.

Regenerate with `node scripts/map-data/build-san-diego.mjs`. Source attribution, geographic bounds, exact feature IDs, model hash, counts, projection, and height assumptions live in `public/data/san-diego/manifest.json`. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL; generator uses Map3D under MIT.
