# San Diego runtime model

This folder contains the static generated San Diego regional and neighborhood models, sharing the Torrey Highlands metric origin. There are no direct subfolders.

- `torrey-highlands.glb` contains sage-green terrain, richer park greens and dry coastal scrub, cream-stucco buildings with terracotta roofs, real OSM streets and paths, deterministic low-poly trees, and two single-mesh selectable landmarks: Westview High School and Torrey Highlands Plaza. Roads and parking keep distinct paved colors. All landmark facade details and roofs are baked into their corresponding base mesh. The GLB uses Meshopt compression with lossless Float32 positions to preserve centimeter gaps between road, shoulder, landcover, and roof layers. It has no textures or runtime map-service dependency.
- `region.glb` contains the expanded northern San Diego coastline, beach/lagoon, mapped roads and settlements, sage terrain, Torrey Pines bluffs and pines, Park Village canyon and creek, and one compact merged static waterfall mesh with low grounded rock outcrops, a connected short cascade, restrained foam and a small pool aligned to the mapped creek. Mainland coastline connectivity and five tidal islands preserve the northern estuary without cross-bank surface streaks. Terrain, landcover, roads and water share exact ground triangle planes. Buildings have terrain-following foundations and level roofs, using the original warm palette and lossless position compression. An exact rectangular hole and flat perimeter allow the immutable original model to load alongside it without overlapping geometry or a raised ground seam. Geographic district markers/cameras and the mapped coverage rectangle live in the expansion manifest; the waterfall has no separate zoom or selection root.
- `INFO.md` is this folder guide.

Regenerate with `node scripts/map-data/build-san-diego.mjs`. Source attribution, geographic bounds, exact feature IDs, model hash, counts, projection, and height assumptions live in `public/data/san-diego/manifest.json`. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL; generator uses Map3D under MIT.

Regenerate only the expansion with `npm run data:san-diego-region`; its source snapshot, metadata, and audit layout are `region-source.osm.json.gz`, `region-manifest.json`, and `region-layout.json` in the matching data folder. Both assets are texture-free and need no live map API at runtime. Torrey Pines includes an intentionally enlarged, approximately 300 m coastal ridge with existing warm rock colors on exposed western facets, descending eastern slopes, and low beach/lagoon terrain. It is integrated into the shared terrain geometry so map surfaces and foundations follow the mountain. The new coastal/canyon relief and small creek waterfall are documented artistic geometry rather than surveyed elevations, while coastline, waterways, streets, and building placements use OSM.
