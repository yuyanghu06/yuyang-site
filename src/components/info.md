# React components

The complete map feature lives under `map/`. Its `map-shell.tsx` is the server-safe dynamic-loading boundary and `map.tsx` is the thin public entry point. Browser-only avatar rendering lives under `avatar-view/`.

Keep browser-only Three.js behavior behind the client boundary. `world-loader.tsx` owns production's solid-white animated-globe entry screen and press-to-continue control; development bypasses it. `avatar-view/avatar-fullscreen.tsx` owns the FaceTime modal's shared slightly zoomed-out camera, scene-isolated lighting, increased source-material emission with reduced specular response for smoother but saturated skin, memory-conscious render lifecycle, visibility suspension, post-compile animation start hook, and per-frame face-state updates; state-specific folders such as `avatar-view/idle/` own their models and animation setup. `avatar-view/fixed-body-animation.ts` is the shared body-lock policy for current and future clips. `avatar-call.tsx` owns the modal/dock interaction shell, viewport-constrained pointer dragging with nearest-corner snapping and responsive edge margins for the docked tile, its lightweight 12 px CSS backdrop blur, minimized-caption visibility, and forwarding of the agent-streaming state that switches the persistent avatar between blinking idle and talking-face states without changing its body loop. The docked shell rejects drag/open handling for every event originating inside `.agent-chat`, so all caption controls remain inline regardless of their individual event guards. It mounts `agent-chat.tsx` only after the avatar has rendered, the experience-start gate is open, and the one-shot startup wave has finished; therefore the initial greeting cannot begin during the wave. `agent-chat.tsx` derives Yuyang's displayed age from the October 29, 2006 birth date, stays mounted across minimizing, owns the FaceTime caption history, streamed agent state, validated server commands, and throttled CC0 dialogue blips, and reports both scripted and server stream lifecycles to the call shell. Its guided-tour state waits for the Manhattan camera to settle, then streams the Manhattan introduction and exposes white Union Square and Washington Square buttons that immediately dismiss the current caption, minimize the guide, and use the authored navigation path. The Washington choice waits for its neighborhood camera to settle and streams the NYU introduction with a blue Next pill. Selecting Next navigates to the authored Lipton Hall close view first; only after that camera settles does the separate Lipton residence caption stream, with another Next pill beneath it. Every scripted or agent-requested pill is rendered inside the latest message card while the guide is minimized and in the normal control area below the card while fullscreen; Reply remains the card's inline action in both modes. The composer flexes its text field around the circular send arrow and a plain blue Cancel text action matching Reply, which restores the caption actions. Production's continue gesture primes the shared dialogue audio before the first greeting. Any stream begun while minimized—or minimized while active—reveals the newest right-aligned caption beside the avatar; it persists after completion until its ×, an outside click, or expansion dismisses it. Completed history and ordinary controls remain hidden in that docked presentation, while the current guided-tour choices stay available inside the latest caption. Its expanded history, scroll bounds, inline Reply/composer behavior, and blue continuation controls remain unchanged.

Agent output is hard-split at natural boundaries into caption segments of at most 170 characters. Overflow remains queued, each blue Next pill reveals one segment, and both the blue Next and final white Cancel presentation pills render beneath the message card. Cancel minimizes the guide.

Docked captions are persistent across outside clicks, fullscreen expansion, and later re-minimization. The explicit × at the top-left dismisses one directly, and every minimized action pill fades the current bubble closed before running its action; maximizing the call never clears caption visibility.

Caption visibility is gated to the shell's complete 460 ms mode transition in both directions. On minimize, the docked bubble remains hidden until the avatar tile settles. On expansion, an explicit `expandedSettled` state holds the fullscreen caption at zero opacity for the complete resize, then starts its 220 ms fade after the modal reaches final size; this prevents the previous docked opacity from carrying into the expanded frame. New streams that begin after the dock settles still reveal immediately, while streams during the transition wait for the same gate.

Minimized action pills—including Yes/No, Okay, destinations, Next, and presentation controls—first fade out the current caption over 180 ms, then run their action. Reply remains visible because it edits the current bubble instead of advancing it. A queued or streamed follow-up explicitly reveals its new caption after the previous bubble has closed.

The expanded history re-applies its latest-caption scroll anchor whenever the call expands, because docked mode intentionally removes the invisible scroll room and cannot establish the expanded default position. Control-state changes also refresh that anchor so the newest card and its pill remain the base view while older turns stay available by scrolling upward.

When only one assistant caption exists, the scroll range is fixed at zero so the initial message stays in its natural unshifted position with its controls beneath it. Latest-caption anchoring begins only after a real multi-message history exists.

The Washington narration is also bound to the settled Washington camera event for scroll-driven entry. It may start once from the expected Washington-arrival phase, the Manhattan destination-choice phase, or free exploration; a ref prevents replay on later returns. Declining the tour and unrelated active scripted phases are not interrupted.
