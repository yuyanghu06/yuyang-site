# React components

Dock placement note: `avatar-call.tsx` tracks the minimized avatar's horizontal and vertical snapped edges so its caption remains inward-facing and aligns with the avatar's bottom edge at either lower corner.

The complete map feature lives under `map/`. Its `map-shell.tsx` is the server-safe dynamic-loading boundary and `map.tsx` is the thin public entry point. Browser-only avatar rendering lives under `avatar-view/`. Markdown-authored camera scripts live under `dialogue/`, grouped into global, Manhattan, and Union Square view families.

Keep browser-only Three.js behavior behind the client boundary. `world-loader.tsx` owns production's solid-white animated-globe entry screen and press-to-continue control; development bypasses it. `avatar-view/avatar-fullscreen.tsx` owns the FaceTime modal's shared slightly zoomed-out camera, scene-isolated lighting, increased source-material emission with reduced specular response for smoother but saturated skin, memory-conscious render lifecycle, visibility suspension, post-compile animation start hook, and per-frame face-state updates; state-specific folders such as `avatar-view/idle/` own their models and animation setup. `avatar-view/avatar-emote-player.ts` expands compact animation-only one-shots against the persistent idle clip without mounting duplicate avatar scenes. `avatar-call.tsx` owns the modal/dock interaction shell, viewport-constrained pointer dragging with nearest-corner snapping and responsive edge margins for the docked tile, its lightweight 12 px CSS backdrop blur, minimized-caption visibility, Escape dismissal, and forwarding of agent-streaming state. Escape marks the caption dismissed before minimizing, preventing it from reappearing beside the docked avatar. The shell rejects drag/open handling for every event originating inside `.agent-chat`, so all caption controls remain inline regardless of their individual event guards. It mounts `agent-chat.tsx` only after the avatar has rendered, the experience-start gate is open, and the one-shot startup wave has finished. `agent-chat.tsx` derives Yuyang's displayed age, owns Markdown-authored guided-tour narration, capped streaming captions, camera navigation, and tour/free-mode Next and Back behavior. Docked caption positioning is independent of its visible class so completing or advancing a caption fades it out in place without a one-frame horizontal jump.

The shared dialogue renderer hard-splits both imported scripted narration and agent output at natural boundaries into caption segments of at most 170 characters. Overflow remains queued, and each blue Next pill streams the next segment through the same character-by-character renderer instead of inserting it instantly, including when a landmark dialogue was opened directly outside the tour. During the guided tour, a gray Back pill sits second: it returns to the prior caption on the current camera when one exists, otherwise it navigates to the preceding camera and restores that stop's last caption. Back is absent outside the guided tour. The final white Cancel presentation pill minimizes the guide.

Docked captions are persistent across outside clicks, fullscreen expansion, and later re-minimization. The explicit × at the top-left dismisses one directly, and every minimized action pill fades the current bubble closed before running its action; maximizing the call never clears caption visibility.

Caption visibility is gated to the shell's complete 460 ms mode transition in both directions. On minimize, the docked bubble remains hidden until the avatar tile settles. On expansion, an explicit `expandedSettled` state holds the fullscreen caption at zero opacity for the complete resize, then starts its 220 ms fade after the modal reaches final size; this prevents the previous docked opacity from carrying into the expanded frame. New streams that begin after the dock settles still reveal immediately, while streams during the transition wait for the same gate.

Minimized action pills—including Yes/No, Okay, destinations, Next, and presentation controls—first fade out the current caption over 180 ms, then run their action. Reply remains visible because it edits the current bubble instead of advancing it. A queued or streamed follow-up explicitly reveals its new caption after the previous bubble has closed.

The expanded history re-applies its latest-caption scroll anchor whenever the call expands, because docked mode intentionally removes the invisible scroll room and cannot establish the expanded default position. Control-state changes also refresh that anchor so the newest card and its pill remain the base view while older turns stay available by scrolling upward.

When only one assistant caption exists, the scroll range is fixed at zero so the initial message stays in its natural unshifted position with its controls beneath it. Latest-caption anchoring begins only after a real multi-message history exists.

The Washington narration is also bound to the settled Washington camera event for scroll-driven entry. It may start once from the expected Washington-arrival phase, the Manhattan destination-choice phase, or free exploration; a ref prevents replay on later returns. Declining the tour and unrelated active scripted phases are not interrupted.
