# React components

The complete map feature lives under `map/`. Its `map-shell.tsx` is the server-safe dynamic-loading boundary and `map.tsx` is the thin public entry point. Browser-only avatar rendering lives under `avatar-view/`.

Keep browser-only Three.js behavior behind the client boundary. `avatar-view/avatar-fullscreen.tsx` owns the FaceTime modal's shared camera, scene-isolated lighting, smiling atlas canvas, memory-conscious render lifecycle, and visibility suspension; state-specific folders such as `avatar-view/idle/` own their models and animation setup. `avatar-view/fixed-body-animation.ts` is the shared body-lock policy for current and future clips. `avatar-call.tsx` owns the modal/dock interaction shell and its lightweight 12 px CSS backdrop blur with a matching localized camera-light highlight, while linearly repositioning and scaling the unchanged avatar canvas when its presentation state changes. The shell does not construct or activate an SVG displacement filter during transitions.
