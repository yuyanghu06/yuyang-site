# React components

The complete map feature lives under `map/`. Its `map-shell.tsx` is the server-safe dynamic-loading boundary and `map.tsx` is the thin public entry point. Browser-only avatar rendering lives under `avatar-view/`.

Keep browser-only Three.js behavior behind the client boundary. `avatar-view/avatar-fullscreen.tsx` owns the FaceTime modal's shared camera, lighting, smiling atlas canvas, and render lifecycle; state-specific folders such as `avatar-view/idle/` own their models and animation setup. `avatar-view/fixed-body-animation.ts` is the shared body-lock policy for current and future clips. `avatar-call.tsx` owns the modal/dock interaction shell and its stable-size `simple-liquid-glass` live-backdrop refraction, while linearly repositioning and scaling the unchanged avatar canvas when its presentation state changes. The glass instance remains mounted across every state. During modal resizing it is hidden beneath a lightweight surface that retains the same 12 px backdrop blur and saturation, avoiding both per-frame SVG refraction and the former end-of-transition filter reconstruction.
