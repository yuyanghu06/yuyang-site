# New York dialogue

This folder groups New York camera dialogue. The Manhattan overview stays at the city root; each neighborhood has its own folder. `../index.ts` exports these scripts to the shared dialogue renderer.

## Subfolders

- `union-square/` contains the Union Square overview and landmark camera scripts.
- `washington-square/` contains the Washington Square overview and landmark camera scripts.

## Files

- `manhattan.md` contains the Manhattan arrival and neighborhood-choice dialogue. Its final caption offers Union Square and Washington Square after the camera settles.
- `INFO.md` documents the New York dialogue hierarchy.
