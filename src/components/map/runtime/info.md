# Map runtime

This folder composes the persistent renderer, geographic navigation, camera framing, visibility lifecycle and resource cleanup. It has no direct subfolders. Keep one renderer across the globe and both cities; do not remount it when changing view.

## Direct files

- `map-runtime.tsx` owns scene setup, input, authored camera transitions, history, validated agent commands, settled-view events and audio state. San Diego prepares its regional and Torrey Highlands models atomically on first request and caches them for return visits. The region exposes three yellow district pins without text labels; Torrey Highlands retains four merged selectable landmarks and the original camera and fixed lighting. Its people/cars mount once, reveal on first Torrey Highlands entry and stop updating when hidden; district changes explicitly hide population before skipping those frames. Static local transforms/bounds and exact shared landmark-glow geometry avoid redundant work. Failed or late loads dispose resources; partial registration failures clear landmarks, picking roots, district markers, annotations and animations for retry. A generation guard prevents a superseded direct load request from switching views. The globe label and leader remain unchanged. San Diego pins use the NYC bob frequency with a four-screen-pixel amplitude at every zoom level. Park Village's waterfall stays noninteractive, and its annotation text is removed. Metadata anchors still support camera framing. A separate San Diego sky reuses NYC overview-only clouds/planes and district bird flocks on fixed physical routes without rendering New York travelers. Its camera shares map framing with a broader depth range for overhead geometry. Torrey Pines alone shows small ocean crests; hidden waves/sky skip animation work. The base San Diego exit zooms to 72% of its starting scale before the globe flight, with a delayed cloud veil. Only that short departure can use the already-generated peripheral terrain bounds, keeping ordinary navigation inside detailed source coverage. San Diego uses main dev's bias (-0.00015), radius (6), zero normal bias and per-light shadow caching; light framing changes invalidate the cache, and the broad overview omits shadows. Hidden New York and globe animation work is skipped during San Diego views. Manhattan loading remains the existing eager main-dev implementation; main-only city/mobile/music changes are outside this merge.
- `dispose-runtime.ts` releases shared geometry, materials, textures and InstancedMesh GPU instance buffers exactly once per cleanup; object-only cleanup handles failed and late lazy loads. The runtime releases San Diego's shadow target separately.
- `landmark-framing.ts` fits all eight bounding-box corners to the authored destination camera with a 7% inset, retaining New York presets and Torrey Highlands' original overview. Its bounds helper supports geographic framing independently of selectable landmarks.
- `coverage-framing.ts` constrains desired and displayed orthographic poses to mapped source coverage, using both limiting content-height planes. It preserves safe authored poses, can keep the waterfall readable and derives depth planes without exposing empty peripheral skirts.
- `navigation-route.ts` chooses one authored stop at a time. San Diego district siblings pass through the region, all four landmark exits pass through Torrey Highlands, and cross-city travel passes through the globe. New York keeps direct neighborhood traversal.
- `san-diego-free-camera.ts` retains disabled authoring controls and pure orbit/pan/zoom/serialization helpers. Production starts from authored camera positions. The base San Diego overview cannot rotate; all three district views and selected landmark close-ups allow horizontal yaw. Rotated landmark framing is recalculated, and coverage constraints remain active.
- `viewport-lifecycle.ts` suspends and resumes animation for page visibility and viewport presence.
- `info.md` documents this folder.
