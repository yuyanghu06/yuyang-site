# Map runtime

This folder composes one persistent renderer, geographic navigation, camera framing, visibility lifecycle and resource cleanup across the globe and both cities. It has no direct subfolders.

## Direct files

- `map-runtime.tsx` owns scene setup, input, authored camera transitions, history, validated agent commands, settled-view events and audio state. New York and San Diego prepare independently on first request and stay cached for return visits. New York dynamically imports its loader/waterfront and shares one first-ready promise until roads, initial tiles and all six landmark registrations finish. San Diego atomically prepares its regional and original Torrey Highlands models with four merged selectable landmarks. Failed or late loads dispose resources; partial registration failures roll back geometry, population, picking, markers and animation state before retry. Generation guards prevent superseded direct loads from switching views, and status/readiness gates classify all San Diego districts and landmarks consistently.
- `dispose-runtime.ts` releases shared geometry, materials, textures and InstancedMesh GPU instance buffers once per cleanup; object-only cleanup handles failed and late lazy loads. Runtime teardown also releases both cities' shadow targets and the separate San Diego sky scene.
- `landmark-framing.ts` fits all eight bounding-box corners to authored destination cameras with a 7% inset, retaining New York presets and Torrey Highlands' original overview. Its bounds helper supports geographic framing independently of selectable landmarks.
- `coverage-framing.ts` constrains desired and displayed orthographic poses to mapped coverage, using both limiting content-height planes. It preserves safe authored poses, can keep the waterfall readable and derives depth planes without exposing empty peripheral skirts.
- `navigation-route.ts` chooses one authored stop at a time. San Diego district siblings pass through the region, landmark exits pass through Torrey Highlands and cross-city travel passes through the globe. New York keeps direct neighborhood traversal.
- `san-diego-free-camera.ts` retains disabled authoring controls and pure orbit/pan/zoom/serialization helpers. Production starts from authored cameras. The base San Diego overview cannot rotate; district views and selected landmark close-ups allow horizontal yaw. Rotated landmark framing is recalculated and coverage constraints remain active.
- `viewport-lifecycle.ts` suspends and resumes animation for page visibility and viewport presence.
- `info.md` documents this folder.

Both cities start their own deferred ambience preload alongside geometry. Torrey Highlands Plaza uses mall ambience, schools use crowd audio, and unlocked San Diego district/overview views use birds; New York retains traffic and neighborhood crowd routing. Shared effects mute, music pause/resume, fades and disposal remain in the central audio controller.

Touch/coarse city views with viewport short-edge at most 1024 use native-bounded DPR 1–1.5, a 60 FPS interaction ceiling and the established 30 FPS settled cadence. San Diego shadows use 1024 on that profile and 2048 on desktop; New York retains 1024. City/viewport/pointer changes refresh quality without restarting camera fitting, and shadow resizing releases both old render targets. Torrey Highlands keeps its original fixed lighting, zero normal bias and four merged landmark cameras; larger district light framing invalidates its independent shadow cache, while the broad regional overview omits shadows.

San Diego's original people/cars mount once, reveal on first Torrey Highlands entry and stop updating while hidden; district changes hide them before skipping those frames. Static local transforms/bounds and shared landmark glow geometry avoid redundant work. Its three yellow district pins have no text labels and bob at the NYC frequency with four-screen-pixel amplitude at every zoom. Park Village's waterfall remains noninteractive. A separate San Diego sky reuses overview-only clouds/planes and district birds on fixed physical routes, while Torrey Pines alone shows small ocean crests. Hidden city/globe animation work is skipped.

The San Diego outbound cloud veil starts immediately and reaches full opacity at 18% of the 2,168 ms transition (about 390 ms). The base view zooms to 72% of its starting scale before globe flight; only this brief departure may use peripheral terrain bounds. Normal navigation remains inside detailed mapped coverage. New York entry waits behind its cloud veil until lazy preparation completes; direct commands and browser history retain their pending destination.
