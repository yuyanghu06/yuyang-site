# Map runtime

`map-runtime.tsx` composes scene setup, navigation, input, and extracted systems behind the thin `../map.tsx` entry. Its globe view applies a restrained idle spin and parents clouds, planes, boats, and both city markers to the same interactive globe transform. Manhattan loading is owned by `../manhattan/loading/`, hit-testing by `../interaction/`, viewport suspension/start-stop by `viewport-lifecycle.ts`, and resource disposal by `dispose-runtime.ts`.

## Files

- `dispose-runtime.ts` disposes shared geometry, material, and texture resources when the map unmounts; its object-only cleanup also handles lazy results that finish after teardown.
- `info.md` is this local ownership guide.
- `landmark-framing.ts` projects all eight merged landmark bounds corners through the destination camera to cap San Diego landmark zoom with a 7% viewport-edge inset. Its bounds helper is separate from geographic coverage: fitting a landmark does not authorize showing empty terrain outside the detailed source rectangle. New York presets remain unchanged.
- `coverage-framing.ts` analytically intersects all four orthographic viewport corners with both limiting content-height planes. Its pure zoom-floor and target-clamp solver keeps the entire visible ground footprint inside manifest `coverageBounds`, excluding empty skirts. It preserves safe authored poses, optionally keeps the Park Village waterfall inside a 15% edge inset, and derives tighter near/far planes from visible ray depths for stable ground-layer precision.
- `san-diego-free-camera.ts` retains the disabled San Diego authoring flag, orthographic orbit/tilt/pan/zoom math, elevation and zoom limits, and displayed-pose JSON serialization. Its flag is false: San Diego uses fixed authored framing and guided inputs, with no authoring controls shown. It has no persistent storage or authored-preset writes.
- `navigation-route.ts` chooses one authored geographic or landmark stop at a time. San Diego district siblings pass through the region; landmark exits first settle at Torrey Highlands. City changes pass through the globe, while New York retains its direct neighborhood traversal.
- `map-runtime.tsx` imports the reorganized agent contracts and JSON-backed camera context, owns the persistent client-side renderer, geographic and landmark view orchestration, validated agent-navigation event handling, settled-view browser notifications, audio-state reporting to the shared site controller, and forwarding of the shell's experience-started gate into the avatar call. It resolves every interactive landmark and its framing from the shared live camera-view registry; agent navigation always uses authored interpolation or the globe cloud flight, and cross-level destinations queue and fully settle at every intermediate camera before advancing instead of cutting camera modes. San Diego is dynamically imported and decoded only on first entry, rendered in a separate scene with matching warm light, and held behind the cloud veil until both regional and original Torrey Highlands models and merged landmark roots are ready. The broad region exposes three labeled district destinations; Park Village has a noninteractive waterfall annotation. District labels retain a readable screen size, hide when their anchors leave the viewport, and clamp visible text inside an 8-pixel inset. Portrait region framing favors the Torrey Highlands/Park Village pair; cropped destinations remain available in the guide. District lighting follows authored centers, retains the original Torrey Highlands light offset, and omits broad-region shadow rendering. Failed loads return to the globe with a retry message. City changes route through the globe, landmarks participate in browser history, and scene visibility plus picking filters prevent overlapping local coordinate systems from mixing. Its bottom-right attribution line switches source credit by city and also exposes the unobtrusive internal link to the crawlable blog.
- `viewport-lifecycle.ts` suspends and resumes animation based on page visibility and viewport presence.
