# Map runtime

San Diego's outbound cloud veil starts immediately and reaches full opacity at 18% of the 2,168 ms globe transition (about 390 ms), covering peripheral map edges early in the pullback.

This folder composes the persistent renderer, geographic navigation, camera framing, visibility lifecycle and resource cleanup. It has no direct subfolders. Keep one renderer across the globe and both cities; do not remount it when changing view.

San Diego geometry preparation starts no ambience downloads. The runtime reports its current view and camera state to the shared audio controller: birds first load and play after an activated San Diego view has settled with an unlocked camera and no globe transition, while mall audio waits for a settled, locked Torrey Highlands Plaza visit. Failed or canceled entry keeps both recordings unloaded; revisits reuse sources already assigned by playback.

## Direct files

- `map-runtime.tsx` owns scene setup, input, authored camera transitions, history, validated agent commands, settled-view events and audio state. San Diego first prepares only its compact overview. Torrey Highlands/landmark navigation requests the untouched original detail, while Torrey Pines/Park Village request the untouched regional detail. Each layer mounts atomically after preparation and caches for return visits; every base-view return hides both full layers and restores the simplified overview. The previous view remains visible while destination detail loads. Direct navigation, tour steps, Back/forward and Escape share readiness gating; generation checks and per-layer cancellation prevent superseded navigation or late decoded results from changing the view. The region exposes three yellow district pins without text labels; Torrey Highlands retains four merged selectable landmarks and the original camera and fixed lighting. Its people/cars and four landmark registrations mount only after original detail succeeds, reveal on first Torrey Highlands entry and stop updating when hidden; district changes explicitly hide population before skipping those frames. Static local transforms/bounds and exact shared landmark-glow geometry avoid redundant work. Failed or late loads dispose resources; partial original registration failures clear landmarks, picking roots, markers and animations for retry without discarding the visible overview. The overview has separate marker/sky rollback. Torrey Pines waves prepare on its first request, including when Park Village already cached regional geometry. A generation guard prevents a superseded direct load request from switching views. The globe label and leader remain unchanged. San Diego pins use the NYC bob frequency with a four-screen-pixel amplitude at every zoom level. Park Village's waterfall stays noninteractive, and its annotation text is removed. Metadata anchors still support camera framing. A separate San Diego sky reuses NYC overview-only clouds/planes and district bird flocks on fixed physical routes without rendering New York travelers. Its camera shares map framing with a broader depth range for overhead geometry. Torrey Pines alone shows small ocean crests; hidden waves/sky skip animation work. The base San Diego exit zooms to 72% of its starting scale before the globe flight, with an immediate, faster cloud veil. Only that short departure can use the already-generated peripheral terrain bounds, keeping ordinary navigation inside detailed source coverage. San Diego uses main dev's bias (-0.00015), radius (6), zero normal bias and per-light shadow caching; light framing changes invalidate the cache, and the broad overview omits shadows. Hidden New York and globe animation work is skipped during San Diego views. New York requests no city assets while the globe is idle. Its first city intent prepares the compact content-hashed building/park overview and exact roads; Washington/Union entry prepares bounded full tiles and the unchanged merged landmarks only when requested. Both city entries hold the fully opaque cloud veil until their prepared overview succeeds; canceled, superseded and failed requests cannot land. New York lands directly at its authored height/radius and zoom 0.3, without the former 0.92-to-0.3 pullback. Waterfront arrivals finish under the veil before exposure. Capture-phase Escape cancels pending city entry or neighborhood/district navigation and consumes only a handled cancellation before avatar-caption dismissal; agent commands, pointer/scroll input and history share readiness gates. Starting a prepare invalidates settled-view reporting synchronously so even a failure or cancellation before the next frame restores the guide for the visible view. The production shell receives readiness after the imported globe or its failure fallback has actually rendered. Partial New York landmark registration also rolls back for retry.
- `dispose-runtime.ts` releases shared geometry, materials, textures and InstancedMesh GPU instance buffers exactly once per cleanup; object-only cleanup handles failed and late lazy loads. The runtime releases San Diego's shadow target separately.
- `landmark-framing.ts` fits all eight bounding-box corners to the authored destination camera with a 7% inset, retaining New York presets and Torrey Highlands' original overview. Its bounds helper supports geographic framing independently of selectable landmarks.
- `coverage-framing.ts` constrains desired and displayed orthographic poses to mapped source coverage, using both limiting content-height planes. It preserves safe authored poses, can keep the waterfall readable and derives depth planes without exposing empty peripheral skirts.
- `navigation-route.ts` chooses one authored stop at a time. San Diego district siblings pass through the region, all four landmark exits pass through Torrey Highlands, and cross-city travel passes through the globe. New York keeps direct neighborhood traversal.
- `san-diego-free-camera.ts` retains disabled authoring controls and pure orbit/pan/zoom/serialization helpers. Production starts from authored camera positions. The base San Diego overview cannot rotate; all three district views and selected landmark close-ups allow horizontal yaw. Rotated landmark framing is recalculated, and coverage constraints remain active.
- `viewport-lifecycle.ts` suspends and resumes animation for page visibility and viewport presence.
