# San Diego map

This folder loads the broad San Diego coastal/canyon region and its Torrey Highlands, Torrey Pines, and Park Village district views into the persistent map runtime. Coordinates are meters from longitude −117.1518, latitude 32.9606, with east +X, north −Z, and up +Y. There are no direct subfolders.

## Direct files

- `load-scene.ts` revalidates both generated manifests and fetches their Meshopt GLBs with content hashes as cache keys. Both scenes must finish before reveal; cancellation and partial failures dispose completed geometry and materials. The original Torrey Highlands overview and shadow policy remain authoritative for that district. Regional shadows use an explicit allowlist of building, tree, and waterfall mesh names; flat terrain, water, beaches, roads, and landcover receive shadows but never cast them. The loader exposes the two original merged selectable landmark roots, regional and district framing bounds, detailed-source `coverageBounds` with conservative content-height limits, attribution, and noninteractive annotations. Coverage excludes peripheral skirts; compatible older manifests use the identical source rectangle in `bounds`. It makes no requests to live geographic services.
- `district-markers.ts` combines the existing yellow destination-pin style with readable canvas labels for the three districts. Its shared label builder also creates the noninteractive Park Village waterfall annotation; labels maintain their screen size in the runtime. Offscreen district/landmark anchors hide, visible text stays within an 8-pixel viewport inset, and the waterfall annotation retains its fixed geographic anchor while its label can shift for readability.
- `road-joins.ts` reconstructs the original street mesh's ribbon quads after decoding and merges small bevel triangles into that same noninteractive mesh to close pale gaps at road bends. Only a unique pair of connected ends with matching width, color, and elevation can join; terminal ends, multiway intersections, lane dashes, and different bridge levels remain intact. Road and cream-shoulder joins stay inside the convex hull of the existing edge endpoints, preserving road widths, bridge-ramp heights, selectable landmark geometry, and the original GLB bytes. It uses no additional source-data request.
- `INFO.md` is this folder guide.

The original generator is `scripts/map-data/build-san-diego.mjs`; source data and provenance are in `public/data/san-diego/` and models are in `public/models/san-diego/`. Regional additions live in the separate region manifest and GLB. The original Torrey Highlands model and manifest are preserved.
