# San Diego map

This folder loads the broad San Diego coastal/canyon region and its Torrey Highlands, Torrey Pines, and Park Village district views into the persistent map runtime. Coordinates are meters from longitude −117.1518, latitude 32.9606, with east +X, north −Z, and up +Y. There are no direct subfolders.

## Direct files

- `load-scene.ts` revalidates the original, regional and derived overview manifests, verifies source provenance, and fetches only the compact overview GLB on initial city entry. Original Torrey Highlands detail loads only for that district or its four landmarks; regional detail loads only for Torrey Pines or Park Village. Full source GLBs, camera presets, unique merged selectable roots and regional shadow policy remain exact. Frozen population tables/instancing code dynamically import only for original detail, with provenance validation before its model request. Static matrices/bounds prepare once, and failed or aborted decodes release resources.
- `scene-lod.ts` owns independently cached original/regional detail, deduplicates requests, and mounts only after successful registration/effect preparation. Superseded detail requests abort; late decoded results dispose. The base overview always renders both simplified groups, even after district visits. Close views replace only their own simplified group with the full source layer and retain the other simplified layer as distant surroundings. Failed preparation leaves the overview intact and permits retry; disposal is idempotent.
- `scene-resources.ts` computes geometry bounds and freezes local transforms except for dynamic merged landmark roots. The default shadow policy retains main dev; an optional mesh predicate restricts regional casters to buildings, trees and the waterfall. Additive landmark glows share each root's exact local geometry, preserving whole-object movement and shared disposal.
- `population.ts` validates frozen spawn provenance against the loaded model and mounts two pedestrian meshes and two car meshes once, when original Torrey Highlands detail succeeds. The population uses shared NYC geometry/materials and reveal animation, and is disposed with the city scene. School campuses hold 145 of 196 people and 40 of 70 cars; neighborhoods hold only 36 people and 24 cars.
- `car-spawn.ts` supplies separate San Diego routes and an ordinary-car palette with no yellow taxis. Cars never reuse NYC traffic placement or taxi colors.
- `INFO.md` is this folder guide.

The generator is `scripts/map-data/build-san-diego.mjs`; source data and provenance are in `public/data/san-diego/` and the model is in `public/models/san-diego/`.

Frozen spawn data lives in `src/generated/san-diego-people-spawns.ts` and `san-diego-car-spawns.ts`. After model regeneration, run the separate `data:san-diego:people` and `data:san-diego:cars` commands (or `data:san-diego:population`). These deterministic generators check mapped geometry offline and sample pedestrian ground support directly from the final GLB. Campus walkers avoid raised walking ribbons, complete cream road shoulders, and road traffic, neighborhood walkers stay on mapped paths at their actual 0.53 m surface, and cars sit on flat 0.5 m road surfaces; neither source nor layout JSON is fetched by the runtime.
- `district-markers.ts` creates yellow district pins without text and constrains their screen size/visibility. Scenery annotation text is not rendered; metadata positions remain available to camera framing.
- `road-joins.ts` retains new dev's optional original-road ribbon repair utility. It is not called by the integrated loader, which preserves main dev's full Torrey Highlands geometry exactly.

Regional additions live in separate region files. Regeneration derives the regional exclusion from the active main-dev manifest so the larger Torrey Highlands patch cannot overlap regional geometry.

- `ocean-waves.ts` samples the full decoded Pacific Ocean surface once on the first Torrey Pines request (Park Village does not prepare waves) and creates up to 96 small curved crests in one InstancedMesh. Every moving crest footprint is checked against ocean geometry; a shader handles drift/fade without per-instance frame updates. Crests are visible only in Torrey Pines, are not selectable and do not cast shadows.
- `sky.ts` reuses NYC low-poly cloud, passenger-plane and cartoon-bird builders in a separate San Diego sky scene. Static cloud/aircraft parts are batched by material, birds retain wing flaps, and each model retains a fixed physical scale and geographic route. Five clouds and two planes prepare with first San Diego loading and appear only in the base overview. Each district owns eight birds on fixed geographic routes. No sky objects derive position or scale from the camera; hidden routes skip updates, and reduced motion freezes movement.
