# Map Experience Milestone

Last updated: 2026-08-18

## Milestone status

The visual direction and Manhattan camera hierarchy are defined and preserved in Figma. The current repository is a Next.js map prototype on the `map-preview` branch. The interaction and LOD decisions below are approved design requirements, not yet a complete production implementation.

## Approved visual direction

- Composition and modeling language: Direction 01.
- Color, light, and atmospheric palette: Direction 07.
- Selected hybrid: Direction 01 composition with Direction 07 color treatment.
- Style intent: a modern National Geographic editorial map with an old-atlas character, rendered as a tactile matte miniature rather than a photorealistic satellite map.
- Selected project asset: `public/style-references/lower-manhattan-selected.png`.

## Manhattan camera hierarchy

1. **View 01 — Arch-visible base:** Washington Square framing with the Washington Square Arch visible immediately; this is the default entry camera.
2. **View 02 — Union + Washington joint view:** intermediate framing that includes both destinations and lets the visitor choose where to continue.
3. **View 03A — Washington Square:** close destination view selected from View 02.
4. **View 03B — Union Square:** close destination view selected from View 02.

## Camera interaction

- The close Washington Square and Union Square views must support drag-to-orbit / re-angle.
- Each destination has a canonical default camera.
- A reset action returns the camera to that destination's default framing.
- Re-angling must remain inside the intended neighborhood experience and must not unlock unrestricted zoom into unrelated parts of the map.

## LOD and geometry constraint

- Every camera level uses the same total polygon count.
- LOD changes by redistributing the fixed polygon budget, not by increasing it.
- Geometry density increases around the active destination as the camera zooms in.
- Distant and off-focus geometry is simplified to release polygons for the active area.
- Re-angling/orbiting must not increase the total polygon budget.
- Transitions should avoid visible topology popping; use stable tier boundaries, hysteresis, or a short morph/fade where necessary.
