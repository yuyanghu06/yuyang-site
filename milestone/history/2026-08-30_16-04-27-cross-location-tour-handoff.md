# Cross-location tour handoff

The guided Union Square route now runs through the full Union Square view, Carlyle Court, and Shift in that order. Its completion offers Washington Square when that location has not already been visited during the current tour.

Completing the Washington Square route at Courant makes the reciprocal Union Square offer under the same condition. The destination is marked visited before navigation, preventing an endless loop after the visitor completes both routes. Visitors can choose Done instead, and the two new Union landmark stops retain guided-tour Next and Back controls.

Targeted ESLint, full TypeScript validation, and whitespace validation pass.
