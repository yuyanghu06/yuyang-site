# Symmetric city memory and mobile final review

Role: orchestrator integration, independent evaluator acceptance, and production verification.

Both cities prepare their own geometry and ambience only when first requested. New York no longer eagerly constructs roads, waterfronts, parks, populations, or landmarks during globe/San Diego visits. Its loader returns readiness after the initial 405 tiles, roads and six landmark registrations; the cloud transition waits for this. San Diego retains its first-entry lazy scene. Visited cities remain cached for fast return; this pass adds no automatic scene eviction after visiting both.

Unused NYC tile/road decoded buffer-view budget is 54,089,590 bytes, plus the 4,440,109-byte runtime JSON and other geometry. The NYC traffic WAV is 18,857,098 authored bytes and remains source-free until NYC is requested. NYC-only visits avoid San Diego geometry and its 5,338,866 audio bytes. These are asset/buffer budgets, not measured total browser RAM.

Touch/coarse city views with a short viewport edge at most 1024 use a native-bounded DPR range of 1–1.5 and a 60 FPS interaction ceiling; idle remains 30 FPS. SD shadow size is 1024 on these devices, versus 2048 on desktop. NYC remains 1024. Desktop and globe limits are preserved, including native DPR below 1. Profile changes reset adaptive samples and clamp immediately; old SD shadow render targets are released before resizing. At 390×844, DPR 2 versus 1.5 is 1,316,640 versus 740,610 pixels (43.75% fewer); shadow texels are reduced 75%. These allocation reductions establish no physical-phone FPS claim.

Implementation handles partial-load rollback, late decoded resource cleanup, retries, direct landmark requests, browser history, rapid destination changes and stale city success/failure status. Shared music mute/pause/resume, effects mute, fades and camera gating retain both main and dev behavior. No geometry, population layouts, authored camera or audio asset was changed.

Verification: independent evaluator reran actual-source lifecycle/navigation/audio and pure profile/adaptive checks, full TypeScript and scoped ESLint/whitespace. No unresolved findings. Full production build passes with all 18 static pages; a second build was required after browser tracing found the legacy root-layout NYC-road preload. Its removal preserves metadata, viewport and body; no other eager road reference remains.

Actual Chrome production request tracing verifies a fresh globe makes no city model/data/ambience requests; SD-only visits make no NYC requests, and NYC-only visits make no SD requests. The first NYC scene loads its 405 tiles and one road request, renders fully, and browser Back/Forward reuses the prepared scene without additional tile requests. Desktop overviews, direct Mesa selection, phone-size NYC overview and a fresh 390×844 SD overview/direct Westview view were visually inspected. Application error logs are empty. Viewport was restored. Device-profile behavior was exercised with controlled capability mocks and a desktop-GPU phone-size standalone renderer; no physical touch device/Safari or overall phone FPS test was performed. Standalone GPU timings were variable, so only pixel/shadow allocation reductions are claimed.

The workspace was externally switched from dev to main with a merge in progress. Four overlapping audio/documentation conflicts were reconciled while preserving both sides; no unmerged files remain. The merge itself remains uncommitted, with other user/agent changes preserved. This pass made no commit, push or deployment. Agent-owned benchmark/proxy/production servers are stopped after review; the user's existing development server is preserved. Diagnostics and request traces are outside the repo under /tmp/yuyang-mobile-optimization/.
