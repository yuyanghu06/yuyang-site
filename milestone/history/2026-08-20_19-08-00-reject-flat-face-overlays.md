# Reject flat face overlays

- Rejected Blender talking v2 because the closed source topology only puckers; the mouth never genuinely opens, and the static eyes read as lifeless.
- Tested a temporary flat animated oval for the mouth and flat eyelid discs, then rejected the approach immediately because the geometry looked pasted onto the face.
- The overlay experiment remained confined to `/tmp` and was removed from the authoring script; it was never saved into the project avatar `.blend` or a review video.
- The next facial pass requires actual oral-cavity/lip topology plus genuine blink and eye-focus controls. No additional full render should be produced until close-up facial tests pass.
