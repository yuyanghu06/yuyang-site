# Globe entry view

- Made an interactive globe the default state in the persistent map renderer.
- Added Natural Earth 1:10m SVG-authored coastlines on a smooth sphere, green land, feathered desert/snow treatments, and a black 3D star field.
- Added globe drag/scroll rotation, a Manhattan destination marker, globe-to-Manhattan flight, browser history support, and Manhattan-to-globe outward navigation.
- Removed raised land displacement, circular pigment blooms, hard biome borders, and triangular terrain marks after visual review.
- Verified ESLint, TypeScript, and the optimized Next.js production build.
