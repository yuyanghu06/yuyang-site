# Idle shoulder-extension review

Created `public/animations/idle-shoulders-extended-review.glb` as a reversible derivative of the protected `idle.glb`; the accepted idle remains byte-for-byte untouched.

The candidate increases each real clavicle-to-upper-arm joint span by 12% and moves the complete corresponding arm, hand, and finger subtree outward. This avoids the ineffective alternative of changing only the disconnected shoulder bone's display tail.

Blender 5.1 validation retains 52 bones, three meshes, one `Idle_Loop` Action over frames 0–60, and loop translation closure within `4.49e-8`. The candidate is unapproved and awaits visual review in the live front orthographic material-preview viewport.
