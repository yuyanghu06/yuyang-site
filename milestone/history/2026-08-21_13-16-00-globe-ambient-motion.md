# Globe ambient motion

Added a restrained eastward idle rotation to the globe, paused during direct globe dragging and geographic transitions. Reused the Manhattan passenger-plane and passenger-boat builders for two faster globe-orbiting planes and three small boats on localized Atlantic/Caribbean water routes. Every ambient traveler is parented to the interactive globe group so manual orbiting and transitions preserve alignment.

Targeted ESLint passes for the runtime, globe-traveler animation, and shared ambient model source. The local site responds successfully, but no connected preview browser was available for an automated visual scale and motion review.
