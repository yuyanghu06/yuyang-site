# Voxel avatar review V4

- The user rejected V1's flat rectangular head, gray helmet-like hair, protruding eyes, mechanical arms, and poor likeness.
- The separate illustrated avatar reference became the facial-art-direction source while the portrait remained authoritative for clothing and color.
- V2 introduced a smaller rounded face and aligned arms but remained too coarse.
- V3 moved the head to a dense approximately 16-by-16 grid and added a true stepped side/rear volume; its first render exposed a feature-depth bug that hid the face.
- V4 moves the eyes, nose, smile, and fringe in front of the curved shell. It also removes the watch, eliminates the sleeve/forearm clipping strip, widens the shoulders, keeps the arms vertical, and replaces fist blocks with a palm, three finger blocks, and a smaller integrated thumb.
- V4 remains unapproved and disconnected from the site. The authoritative production avatar and accepted animations are unchanged.
