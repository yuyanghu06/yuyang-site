# Variable Talking Overlay

- Withdrew the proposed side-to-side neutral arm swing; neutral arms stay relaxed and essentially still.
- Defined talking as a separate mouth-only layer over the exact continuously playing neutral-idle body loop.
- This makes talking duration arbitrary and prevents body-phase jumps when streamed responses begin or end.
- Commas and sentence-ending punctuation schedule closed-mouth pauses; the mouth layer always activates and deactivates on closed frames.
