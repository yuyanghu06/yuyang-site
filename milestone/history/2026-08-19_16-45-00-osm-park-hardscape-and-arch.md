# OSM park hardscape and arch

- Added a reproducible OpenStreetMap crop for Washington Square Park.
- Rendered 134 mapped footway, path, and pedestrian segments plus the mapped fountain footprint; no grass, trees, or other greenery was added.
- Replaced the oversized procedural placeholder with a low-detail extruded arch based on OSM way `248166269`, including its footprint, orientation, correct north-gate coordinates, and mapped 20.5-meter height.
- Identified a downloadable Washington Square Park model on Sketchfab under CC BY as a possible later source, but did not import its greenery-heavy 58.2k-triangle scene.
- ESLint and the optimized production build pass.
