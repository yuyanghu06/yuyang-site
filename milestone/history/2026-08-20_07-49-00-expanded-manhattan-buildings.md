# Expanded Manhattan building coverage

- Expanded the official NYC CityGML crop from 1,250 to 1,800 meters around Washington Square.
- Increased coverage from 8,874 to 14,108 buildings and rebuilt 334 spatial Meshopt GLB tiles in the established deterministic warm palette.
- The compressed building tiles total about 11.1 MiB and now cover the zoomed-out Manhattan camera footprint instead of exposing the former square crop edge.
- Regenerated the frozen ambient layout against the expanded runtime building footprints; accepted counts remain 184 trees, 600 pedestrians, 240 vehicles, and 1,200 park-path segments.
