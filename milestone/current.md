# Current Project Milestone

Last updated: 2026-08-19 19:49 PDT

This is the authoritative rolling project state. Historical session handoffs are stored as timestamped, append-only files in `milestone/history/`.

## Milestone status

The previous multi-scale atlas and Manhattan camera sequence have been retired. The project is restarting from a single Washington Square study so its visual language can be corrected before any broader website structure is rebuilt.

## Current visual direction

- Render Washington Square and its immediate surrounding neighborhood as a restrained 3D miniature.
- Buildings explicitly selected by the user will eventually receive custom recognizable 3D models.
- All other context buildings use the official NYC 2014 CityGML roof, wall, and ground surfaces with a deterministic, higher-depth sun-faded Village palette spanning limestone (`#B9A98D`), sandstone, dusty brick, terracotta, sage, cool gray stone, aged cream, and dark brown-gray. Roofs are lighter and less saturated than their corresponding walls for clearer massing.
- Three verified buildings bypass the procedural palette through BIN-specific overrides: One Fifth Avenue (`1008847`) uses pale weathered gray stone, the NYU Silver Center complex (`1008820`) uses light gray-beige masonry, and Lipton Hall (`1008875`) retains its earlier muted red-brown brick palette. Each has a distinct roof treatment. Lipton additionally carries 447 dark window instances stacked floor-by-floor across 14 eligible CityGML facade faces.
- Washington Square is now being populated with up to 88 deterministic low-poly trees using the exact Courant garden trunk/crown geometry and materials. Placement stays inside the park study area and maintains clearance from mapped paths, the fountain, the arch, and neighboring trees.
- Roughly 18% of Washington Square trees are deterministically selected as taller specimens, with height scales between 1.45× and 2×; the remaining trees receive subtler scale variation. Tree footprints are included in pedestrian route validation to prevent trunk clipping.
- Washington Square crowns now vary independently in width, depth, and vertical fullness across a 0.68–1.36 base scale range. Five softly varied Apple-inspired sage greens replace the earlier uniform dark canopy while retaining the shared Courant low-poly tree geometry.
- The mapped fountain footprint now drives a simple low-detail 3D fountain at its exact OSM center and diameter: muted limestone base and rim, recessed blue-gray water, and a small central pedestal/bowl.
- The fountain includes eight lower pale-blue arcing sprays plus a tapered central geyser roughly 1.7 times the mapped basin radius in height, matching the tall dominant plume of the real Washington Square fountain. Animated droplets flow continuously along the side arcs.
- The map now loads `public/models/washington-square-arch.glb`, isolated from the existing Blender Sketchfab scene as nine meshes. All imported materials are overridden with warm white, the model is normalized to the mapped 20.5-meter height, and it uses OSM way `248166269` for its exact north-gate position and rotation. A visibly open procedural model remains only as a load-failure fallback.
- CityGML BIN `1088400` is excluded from context massing because its solid block occupied the same footprint and hid the detailed Blender arch.
- The imported arch material renders double-sided because several Sketchfab cap faces use reversed/single-sided normals; this keeps the upper section visually solid from every camera rotation.
- The approved Washington Square Arch asset now uses the original detailed nine-mesh Sketchfab geometry with one pristine flat stone lid joined directly over its real Blender crown opening. It exports as the single upright `WashingtonArchOriginalFlatTop_Final` object; the earlier simplified corrected copy and all runtime cap/axis workarounds have been removed.
- Mapped crosswalks are being rebuilt from the OSM crossing nodes plus measured planimetric asphalt spans. The new renderer uses thinner, closer muted stripes sized to each street, and the extraction now rejects the erroneous crossings several blocks west of Washington Square.
- Crosswalks now come from tagged OSM `highway=crossing` nodes and inherit their orientation from connected footway geometry; all earlier hand-estimated crossing coordinates were removed.
- The downloadable CC BY Sketchfab scene `Washington Square Park v03` by `nyu_grad_alley_2020` is now imported into `assets/blender/nyu-custom-buildings.blend` as the style source for Bobst, Courant, and Stern. Bobst's missing rear wall has been closed with a cleaned mirror of its modeled five-bay front facade, retaining the source masonry, glazing, and opening materials without duplicating side-wall trim.
- Corrected Bobst is exported as the 127 KiB `public/models/bobst-library.glb`, replaces CityGML BIN `1008626`, and is scaled to its authoritative footprint and height. Its yaw follows the long Washington Square South facade edge; a CityGML-only fallback remains for GLB load failure.
- Courant and Stern retain their authoritative CityGML massing instead of receiving separate Blender models. Courant is the narrow BIN `1008627` tower. The grouped Stern selection uses the adjacent white BIN `1078952` and red BIN `1077346` masses. Their CityGML surfaces and procedural facade/roof details are baked into one mesh per interaction target before loading; the background tiles omit those BINs to prevent duplicate geometry. The unrelated BIN `1008629` no longer receives the generated window grid.
- The red/gray Stern palettes are assigned by building identity: 40 West 4th Street uses muted red masonry, while the rotunda-bearing 44–50 West 4th Street mass uses cool gray stone. The rotunda has a curved blue-glass band and a tall purple banner whose top edge attaches to an angled pole and whose full 5.2-unit length hangs directly downward.
- Gould Plaza has a lightweight close-view detail pass with raised paving, five low-poly trees, and nine instanced seats. The complete plaza composition is rotated `-0.57` radians to run parallel with the nearest official street edge, and its paving is reduced from `29×22` to `26×18` so it remains inside the plaza instead of clipping the road. The large rectangular Stern/Berkeley roof has four oversized neutral-gray square HVAC housings, each capped by a smaller protruding black square fan block. True polygon-area ranking anchors them to the broad flat roof instead of the thin parapet surface, and the complete assemblies sit 0.75 scene units above the roof datum for clearer vertical exposure.
- Courant's open east forecourt wedge now contains a narrow raised sage planting bed with seven staggered mature low-poly trees. The earlier west-gap placement was removed; every new tree center is verified outside both CityGML building footprints and official roadbeds.
- The orthographic camera uses a tighter crop and a 30% lower elevation. Horizontal click/touch dragging rotates around Washington Square; pan, wheel zoom, pinch zoom, and alternate view navigation remain disabled.
- Previous generated style-reference images and the former Manhattan view hierarchy are no longer authoritative for the new implementation.

## Current codebase

- Branch: `map-preview`.
- Framework: Next.js 16, React 19, TypeScript, and Three.js.
- `/` now renders `src/components/nyc-3d-map.tsx` directly.
- `src/components/atlas-map.tsx` has been removed.
- `src/components/nyc-3d-map.tsx` contains one Washington Square scene only.
- `src/app/globals.css` has been replaced with a minimal stylesheet for the single study.
- The initial orthographic camera looks north from directly south of the square, keeping west on the left and east on the right, and can rotate horizontally around the square.
- Lighting now adapts Figma Direction 05 with reduced cool ambient fill, a stronger warm directional key, reduced exposure, firmer soft shadows, and cream haze beginning farther from the foreground to preserve building contrast. Exact token extraction remains blocked by the Figma Starter-plan MCP limit.
- Official planimetric roadbeds use dark brown-gray (`#6F6A61`) to make the street network more legible against the deeper warm buildings and cooler sidewalk datum.
- The top title/instruction overlay has been removed so the map occupies the canvas without a banner.
- A cool gray ground datum sits 0.56 scene units above the dark roadbeds to create a subtle but readable sidewalk curb; authoritative sidewalk polygons remain a later data refinement.
- The WebGL renderer explicitly requests a stencil buffer so the raised sidewalk datum is cut away over official roadbed polygons instead of covering the asphalt.
- Eight low-contrast off-white zebra crossings mark the two adjoining streets at each Washington Square corner. Their centers are verified to fall inside official roadbed polygons so the raised sidewalk mask does not hide them.
- Crosswalks no longer depend on stencil state. Their compact geometry sits at 0.76 scene units, just above the 0.68 sidewalk datum, which guarantees that the paint remains visible over both the asphalt depth and curb edge.
- Lighting remains a single fixed warm daytime treatment; user-local time-of-day lighting has not yet been implemented.
- Clouds are intentionally absent at the current Washington Square zoom level.
- Sixteen small birds are dispersed across independent overhead lanes, including two near-camera lanes that cover the lower-left projection, with animated wing flaps, gentle vertical drift, varied scale and speed, and seamless off-frame wraparound. White birds use the original long-winged silhouette; gray birds use a distinct pigeon model with a rounder body and head, short tan beak, broad wings, and compact tail.
- A deterministic ambient population adds 300 low-detail instanced pedestrians across non-road, non-building ground. Every complete circular walking route is sampled with clearance probes against road and CityGML building footprints before acceptance; walkers use varied clothing, orientation changes, and a subtle bob while remaining only two render draws.
- A 240-vehicle low-detail instanced fleet keeps roughly 100 cars readable in the overview after camera cropping and building occlusion. Cars use verified straight routes along the principal axes of elongated official roadbed polygons. Center and side probes across every route remain within the source road polygon, outside holes, and clear of CityGML footprints; car geometry is aligned to its travel vector. The fleet samples evenly across the full spatially ordered route list so traffic covers all map directions, and cars reverse continuously at validated vector endpoints rather than teleporting to their starting positions. Strong light/dark body contrast keeps the fleet legible at the overview camera distance. Exactly every third vehicle is taxi yellow.
- Bobst Library, Courant, the grouped Stern pair, and windowed Lipton Hall are discoverable interactive landmarks. Edge outlines have been removed entirely and replaced by a persistent animated enchantment-style surface glow that cycles violet-to-cyan and pulses in intensity, becoming stronger on hover or selection. Hover raises the complete merged target 4.5 scene units, but selection grounds it and eases into a substantially closer (`2.41x`, about 40% above the former `1.72x`) fixed view for every landmark. Courant uses a north-facing azimuth (`0`) with its target shifted 10 units west and 24 units south to frame Courant Plaza and Gould Plaza together. Stern uses the requested southwest-facing azimuth (`Math.PI / 2 + 0.42`) and has a dedicated camera height of `252`, 20% above the other non-Lipton landmark height of `210`. Bobst retains its south/front-side azimuth (`Math.PI - 0.42`), and Lipton retains its approved western-facing azimuth (`0`) and height `273`. Scroll-in over a hovered landmark triggers selection. Scroll-in elsewhere now runs a subtler, faster 260 ms `1.035x` rejection pulse anchored toward the pointer's ground position and returns exactly to the overview. Its gesture lock uses that same 260 ms duration, so input unlocks when the visual pulse finishes. Escape or scroll-out exits selection, restores the overview, and unlocks rotation. Courant, Stern, and Lipton's merged CityGML shells render double-sided so source faces remain visible regardless of winding after the base and higher-LOD geometry are baked together.
- Landmark glow is exclusive during selection: the selected target keeps its animated violet/cyan pulse while every other selectable landmark glow fades completely to zero until Escape or scroll-out clears selection, at which point their idle glow resumes.
- Only windowed Lipton Hall BIN `1008875` receives the interactive treatment. Its authoritative base shell and 447 windows are baked into one mesh, its bottom/ground surface is gray, and its BIN is omitted from regenerated background tiles to prevent duplicate geometry. The adjacent tower remains ordinary context geometry.
- Pedestrian and traffic route validation now uses a 64-meter spatial footprint index instead of scanning roughly 3,000 CityGML footprints for every clearance probe. This removes the multi-billion-check client startup path that could freeze the page and cause severe allocation pressure.
- Structured `[WashingtonSquare load]` client logs report elapsed time, payload sizes, dataset counts, geometry phases, Arch/Bobst GLB completion, and load failures. Three.js `Timer` and `PCFShadowMap` replace the deprecated `Clock` and `PCFSoftShadowMap` APIs.
- Runtime CityGML parsing and triangulation have been removed. `scripts/build-washington-city-tiles.mjs` preprocesses all 3,090 buildings into 64 spatial, front-face-normalized, Meshopt-compressed GLB tiles plus a compact runtime footprint/detail file. The generated tile set is about 3.1 MiB and loads progressively nearest-first in batches of six.
- Tile URLs carry a content version derived from both the CityGML source and generator code, preventing immutable-cache staleness after palette or geometry changes. Tile GLBs receive one-year immutable cache headers; metadata is revalidated.
- Rendering now caps initial DPR at 1.25 (1.0 on lower-core devices), adapts resolution from measured render cost, uses a tighter 1024 shadow map, excludes moving crowds/traffic from shadow casting, renders ambient motion at 30 FPS when idle, and suspends entirely when hidden or off-screen.
- The static 1024 city shadow map is now demand-rendered instead of being regenerated on every 30 FPS ambient frame; it refreshes only as tiles/custom models arrive or while a hover-lifted landmark physically moves. Landmark raycasting also reuses a stable root array and direct root lookup instead of allocating and linearly searching on every pointer event.
- Nearest-first city-tile downloads now begin before synchronous pedestrian, traffic, and landmark construction, overlapping network/decode with startup CPU work. Stable `/data/*.json` assets receive a one-day browser cache with one-week stale-while-revalidate; versioned tile GLBs retain one-year immutable caching.
- All runtime data and GLB requests use one abort signal, and full scene geometry/material disposal remains in cleanup. The first meaningful city-tile batch triggers a 900 ms map fade-in with a reduced-motion fallback.
- `public/data/washington-square-citygml.json` is a tracked 9.35 MiB crop of official NYC CityGML delivery area 12: 3,090 buildings, 59,414 surfaces, and 293,642 vertices within 680 meters of Washington Square.
- `public/data/washington-square-planimetrics.json` contains 555 tracked roadbed polygons from the official NYC 2022 Planimetric Database, providing measured road shapes and widths.
- `public/data/washington-square-addresses.json` joins current NYC Property Address Directory 26B records to CityGML buildings by BIN: 3,007 matched BINs and 4,152 unique addresses.
- Complete downloads and temporary conversion files are gitignored under `data/raw/` and `data/work/`; only cropped runtime data is tracked.
- Repeatable data scripts are `scripts/extract-washington-citygml.py`, `scripts/build-washington-address-index.py`, and `scripts/build-washington-planimetrics.mjs`.
- `scripts/build-washington-park.mjs` reproducibly crops OSM park paths, the fountain, and the Washington Square Arch footprint into tracked `public/data/washington-square-park.json` under the ODbL.
- `TODO.md` records later prototype cleanup and a future indexable personal blog intended to strengthen Yuyang's name SEO through author schema, canonical metadata, sitemap/RSS, social previews, and sustainable article publishing.
- The old atlas, Manhattan overview, Washington/Union joint view, Union Square view, camera transitions, pins, and interactive camera controls are absent from the runtime.
- ESLint, TypeScript, and the optimized production build pass after the performance and pointer-anchored repeat zoom changes. The production route remains statically prerendered.
