# Blender sources

This directory contains editable Blender sources for the globe, custom buildings, and avatar.

The authoritative avatar base is `yuyang-avatar-hand-rig-v2.blend`, a 54-bone rig. Its current authored clips are `yuyang-avatar-idle-loop-v2.blend` and `yuyang-avatar-talking-loop-v2.blend`. Do not resume from older static, unrigged, rejected-hand, or experimental assets.

Landmark detail geometry must be baked or merged with its base building before it becomes selectable or animated.
